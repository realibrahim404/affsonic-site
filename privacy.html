Privacy policy

Last updated 22 October 2026

AffSonic is a self-hosted affiliate publishing tool operated by MD. IBRAHIM ISLAM, trading as AffSonic ("we", "us") in Bangladesh. It runs on our own computer and is not offered as a public service: there is no customer sign-up, and the only accounts it connects to are the ones we authorise ourselves. This policy explains what data the software accesses and what happens to it.

Data we access

Source What is accessed Why
Pinterest The connected account's username and account type, its list of boards, and the Pins AffSonic itself creates, together with their statistics. Granted scopes are user_accounts:read, boards:read, pins:read and pins:write. To create Pins on the connected account and report how they performed.
Affiliate programmes Public product data — titles, prices, images and affiliate links. To build the posts.
Other publishing channels The credentials we supply for our own Medium or WordPress accounts. To publish the same content there.

AffSonic does not access other Pinners' private boards or Pins, does not read anyone's Pinterest inbox or followers, and does not run advertising campaigns.

Where it is stored

All of it is stored in a database file on the computer running AffSonic. OAuth access and refresh tokens, API keys and channel passwords are encrypted at rest. Nothing is uploaded to a hosted AffSonic service, because there isn't one.

Who it is shared with

We do not sell, rent or trade any of this data. It is sent to third parties only here:

  • Pinterest — the Pins, images and text we publish, through the official Pinterest API.
  • Anthropic — product titles and descriptions are sent to the Claude API to draft post copy. Account credentials are never included.
  • Medium and WordPress — only when we have connected those channels, and only the content we publish to them.

Each of those services handles the data it receives under its own privacy policy. We disclose data to anyone else only where the law requires it.

How long it is kept

Products, drafts and published posts are kept until we delete them. Analytics are kept for as long as the installation is configured to retain them. Tokens are kept until the account is disconnected.

Revoking access and deletion

  • Revoke AffSonic's access to a Pinterest account at any time from Pinterest's own security settings. AffSonic can then no longer read or publish to it.
  • Disconnecting an account inside AffSonic deletes the stored tokens for it from the local database.
  • To ask about data held about you, or to request its deletion, email support@affsonic.com. We reply within 30 days.

Affiliate disclosure

Posts published by AffSonic contain affiliate links, and carry a disclosure saying so. We may earn a commission on qualifying purchases made through them.

Children

AffSonic is not intended for use by anyone under 13, and we do not collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes we update the date at the top of this page. Material changes will be described here rather than made silently.
